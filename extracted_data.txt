3. Offers and Discounts
OFFER01 – Welcome Offer
New customers receive 10% off their first order. Maximum discount I300. Cannot be combined with another offer.
OFFER02 – T-shirt Combo
Buy any 2 T-shirts and receive I100 off the combined T-shirt price. Both items must be in the same order.
OFFER03 – Accessories Bundle
Buy any 2 accessories and receive 5% off the lower-priced accessory.
OFFER04 – Free Shipping
Free standard shipping on orders of I1,499 or more. Orders below I1,499 have a demo shipping fee of I79.
Offer rules
Only one promotional offer can be applied per order unless an offer explicitly says otherwise. Discounts are calculated on
eligible product prices before shipping. The welcome offer requires a new customer account. Offers are fictional and may
be changed in a real shop.
4. Shipping and Delivery Policy
• Standard delivery estimate: 3–7 business days after order confirmation.
• Dispatch estimate: within 1–2 business days after successful payment.
• Shipping fee: I79 for orders below I1,499; free standard shipping for orders of I1,499 or more.
• Delivery estimates are not guarantees and may vary by PIN code, courier and public holidays.
• Customers should provide a correct phone number and delivery address at checkout.
• The chatbot must ask the customer to check PIN-code serviceability at checkout rather than promise delivery to every
location.
5. Return, Replacement and Refund Policy
Return window
Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery for eligible products.
Eligible condition
Items must be unused, unwashed, undamaged and returned with original tags and packaging.
Non-returnable items
Personalised products and items marked final sale are not eligible for return, unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.
Wrong or damaged item
Customers should contact support within 48 hours of delivery and provide the order ID and clear photos.
Refund processing
After the returned item is received and inspected, approved refunds are initiated within 5–7 business days. Bank or
payment-provider processing may take additional time.
Return shipping
For an approved wrong or damaged item, the shop covers return shipping. For other eligible returns, the customer pays
return shipping.

8. RAG Assistant Instructions
• Answer using information found in this knowledge base. Do not invent product details, prices, sizes, stock counts or
policy terms.
• When possible, mention the product ID so the customer can identify the exact item.
• If the requested information is missing, say that it is not listed and suggest contacting customer support.
• Treat prices and stock quantities as demo snapshot values. For a production shop, retrieve current price and stock from
the live product database.
• If a customer asks for a recommendation, ask a short clarifying question when needed, such as preferred category,
budget, size or colour.
• Do not claim that an order has been placed, cancelled, refunded or shipped. The chatbot in this project is informational
unless connected to an authorised order-management system.
